# Original aviation housing prompts

Generated on 2026-10-15 with the built-in image generation tool, then copied
unchanged into this project. Both are original synthetic instrument housings;
no photograph, manufacturer logo or museum artwork is included.

## brass.png

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: photorealistic vintage aircraft instrument housing texture for a working radio signal meter.
Primary request: one authentic-looking 1930s aircraft gauge: circular glass instrument in a square aged brass mounting panel, seen EXACTLY straight on, orthographic, absolutely no perspective. The entire square panel fills the square image edge to edge. Four realistic small slotted screws near the four corners, lightly tarnished burnished warm brass with tiny scratches, an inset thick rounded concentric brass bezel, dark glass with subtle peripheral reflection and a perfectly blank nearly black dial. Beautiful real photographed materials, restrained highlights, rich warm brass, professional museum product photograph, sharp focus everywhere.
Composition: circular dial centered EXACTLY at image center, circular clear black dial radius 34 percent of image width; brass bezel occupies radii 35 to 45 percent; square panel reaches all edges. Uniform blank matte charcoal dial inside circle: no marks, no numerals, no text, NO NEEDLE, NO central hub. Software adds the calibrated scale and live pointer later. Glass reflections only at outer rim, keep central face clear and uniformly dark for white scale markings. No additional objects, no external background, no labels, no logos, no watermark. Square high resolution image. This is a realistic photographic texture asset, not vector art or a cartoon.
