# Bakelite photo adaptation prompts

Prepared with built-in image generation on 2026-10-15 from the two user-supplied
VU-meter photographs. Final images: `square.png` and `round.png`. These retain
the reference housing shapes and material character, with blank faces for
calibrated runtime print and live needles. Manufacturer logos and audio VU
markings are removed. The reference photos are not separately bundled.

The subsequent typography, tick style, red arc and thin dark live pointer
preserve the original VU appearance while changing the gradation to S/dBm.
